FAQ

Good to know.

Still have questions? Message us — we're happy to help in English or French.

Do I need experience to start? +
Not at all. Most of our members started as complete beginners. Our coaches meet you exactly where you are — come as you are.
What should I bring? +
Just comfortable clothes, water and a towel. We have gloves and wraps you can borrow for your first sessions.
Can I try a class before joining? +
Yes — drop in for a single class any day, or ask us about a free first-timer session. No commitment needed.
How does the DTV visa work? +
Enrol in a long-term training program and we'll provide an enrolment letter and training confirmation to support your DTV application — letting you stay in Thailand up to 5 years.
Do you rent scooters? +
Yes, we offer scooter rental on site so you can stay mobile during your training season — no separate shop needed.
