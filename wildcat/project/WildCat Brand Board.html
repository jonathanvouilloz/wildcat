01 — Brand essence

WildCat isn't a hardcore fight camp. It's a garden, a pool and a ring where anyone — first-timer, traveller or long-stay nomad — trains real Muay Thai and is treated like family. Run by a woman, built on warmth, not intimidation.

WildCat n'est pas un camp d'entraînement intensif. C'est un jardin, une piscine et un ring où chacun — débutant, voyageur ou nomade installé sur la durée — pratique le vrai Muay Thai et se sent accueilli comme en famille.

02 — Photography & art direction

Sun, sweat & green.

The look is candid and golden, not studio-glossy. Shoot the real place: morning light through the canopy, the pool reflecting the ring, hands being wrapped, a coach laughing with a student. This is what already lives on her Instagram — we just make it consistent.

Hero reference · the open-air gym, pool & garden — Chiang Mai

Do

  • Natural daylight, warm white balance, soft shadows
  • Real members & trainers — diverse, all ages, smiling
  • Show the garden, pool & tropical greenery as backdrop
  • Candid action & quiet moments between rounds

Don't

  • Dark, gritty "warrior" gym shots or cold blue tones
  • Aggressive, ego-driven posing or bloodied imagery
  • Heavy filters, fake HDR or oversaturated neon
  • Stocky, generic fitness-model photography

04 — Iconography & graphic elements

The tiger, and a few marks of our own.

The tiger head is the heart of the brand — keep it as the mascot mark. Around it, a small kit of simple graphic devices keeps everything feeling like WildCat without redrawing the tiger every time.

The master mark

Use the full lock-up where there's room. On busy photos or dark surfaces, give it a solid safe-area.

Clear space — keep min. 1× the tiger height clear

Graphic devices

Simple, reproducible elements — built from the brand colours, no complex illustration needed.

Usage

Stripes section off content & frame photos. The claw slash works as a small accent on buttons or active links. The round stamp suits merch, story stickers and the DTV badge family.

05 — The DTV Visa pillar

Train long-term in Thailand — visa ready.

Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) lets foreigners stay up to 5 years for activities like Muay Thai training. It's a real demand driver for expats & nomads — so it becomes a named pillar of the brand, with its own badge and message.

DTV
Visa Friendly
Soft-power · Muay Thai
Badge for site header, footer & story sticker
1

Enrol in a long-term program

Pick a monthly or multi-month training plan — your qualifying activity for the DTV.

2

Get your documents

We provide an enrolment letter & training confirmation to support your application.

3

Apply & settle in

Stay up to 5 years (180 days per entry). Train, recover, repeat — Chiang Mai is home.

Le DTV permet aux étrangers de rester jusqu'à 5 ans en Thaïlande pour s'entraîner au Muay Thai. WildCat fournit les documents d'inscription pour appuyer la demande — un argument fort pour les expats et nomades.
