Wildcat is run by a woman — Meaw — and on most mornings the ring is half women, half men, all levels mixed. So when someone asks *"is muay thai okay for women?"*, the honest answer from here is: it was never a question.

## Your first session, honestly

Nobody throws you into sparring. Your first class is stance, footwork and pad work at your own pace, with someone next to you who started exactly the same way. Gloves and wraps are free to borrow.

## What the training actually does

Muay thai builds real, practical strength — hips, core, shoulders — and a kind of calm confidence that follows you out of the gym. Most women who stay a month tell us the same thing: the body changes were nice, the headspace changes were better.
