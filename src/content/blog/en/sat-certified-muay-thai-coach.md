## Who issues the license: the Sports Authority of Thailand

The Sports Authority of Thailand (SAT) is the government body for sport in Thailand. Professional boxing, Muay Thai included, falls under its Boxing Sport Commission, and that office runs the coach courses. News reports on earlier sessions describe the same issuer: The Nation wrote in 2024 that trainers attained "a Level C License from the Office of the Boxing Sports Commission, Sports Authority of Thailand".

Any gym can print a nice diploma for its own trainers. This one comes from the state, with a name and a date on it.

## Where the C-License sits among the levels

SAT doesn't publish a page we could find listing every level and what each one allows. Some gyms mention a B level, but we haven't seen it in a source we trust. Here is what the sources do say:

- An introductory course comes first. In February 2026, SAT ran a five-day "Muaythai Instructor Course (Introductory Level) for Foreigners" in Phuket. Thairath reported that graduates "can build on this foundation by applying for higher-level Muay Thai instructor courses (C License)".
- The C-License course is selective. A 2025 C-License session in Phuket ran for ten days and took "50 candidates who passed the qualification criteria", according to The Thaiger. Ours also ran ten days, from 11 to 20 November.
- Senior instructors hold the A-License. The same Thairath report says the introductory course was taught by "over 10 expert instructors holding A Licenses".

## How to check a coach before you book a camp

Whatever camp you pick, these questions take two minutes on WhatsApp:

- Ask to see the certificate. A real one names the person, the course, the dates and the issuer. An SAT certificate carries the SAT logo and the Ministry of Tourism and Sports.
- Ask who teaches the class you'll take. A camp can have one certified coach and five assistants. That's normal, but you should know.
- Don't mix up a fight record and a coaching license. Great fighters are not always great teachers, and the reverse is true too. The license tests the teaching.

## FAQ: Muay Thai coach certification in Thailand

### Who certifies Muay Thai coaches in Thailand?

The Sports Authority of Thailand, through its Office of the Boxing Sport Commission, under the Ministry of Tourism and Sports. Other bodies run their own programmes, such as IFMA for amateur Muay Thai, but the SAT license is the state one.

### What is a C-License in Muay Thai?

It is a Muay Thai coach license from the Sports Authority of Thailand. It comes after an introductory course, and senior instructors hold the A-License above it. Ours took a ten-day course.

### Do I need a certified coach to train in Thailand?

No. You can train anywhere. A certificate simply lets you check that a coach followed the official course. For a long stay on a DTV visa, it can also add weight to your file.

### Are all Wildcat coaches certified?
