### H2: The DTV visa — what it costs and how it changes the math
*Covers PAA: "What is the cost of a Muay Thai course for a DTV visa?" and "How much is the DTV visa for Muay Thai?"*

Key numbers:
- DTV visa fee: 10,000 THB (issued at Thai embassy/consulate abroad)
- Proof of funds required: 500,000 THB in your bank account (does not need to be spent)
- Qualifying Muay Thai course: varies by camp — at Wildcat, the enrolment + training documentation is bundled into the monthly training package (no hidden DTV surcharge)
- ED visa comparison: cheaper upfront but requires ~4 course renewals/year = hidden costs + reporting obligations

Over 1 year on a DTV vs ED visa, the DTV typically wins on total cost and simplicity for serious long-stayers.

Internal links: `/dtv-visa`, `/dtv-visa/muay-thai`, `/dtv-visa/how-to-apply`
