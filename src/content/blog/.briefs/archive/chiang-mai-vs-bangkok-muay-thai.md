### H2: Cost breakdown — real 2026 numbers
*Covers PAA: "How much does Muay Thai training cost in Chiang Mai vs Bangkok?"*

**Chiang Mai (Wildcat):**
- Drop-in: 400 THB
- Monthly training: from 8,000 THB
- Room near camp: 6,000-10,000 THB/month
- Food: 6,000-9,000 THB/month
- Scooter: 3,000 THB/month
- **Total: ~25,000-35,000 THB/month**

**Bangkok (Fairtex / Yokkao area):**
- Monthly training: 12,000-20,000 THB
- Room near gym: 12,000-20,000+ THB/month (Bangkok real estate premium)
- Food: 8,000-14,000 THB/month
- BTS/transport: 2,000-4,000 THB/month
- **Total: ~40,000-65,000 THB/month**

Bangkok costs roughly 40-60% more per month than Chiang Mai for equivalent training. Over a 6-month stay, that gap is 120,000-180,000 THB — roughly the DTV proof-of-funds requirement.
