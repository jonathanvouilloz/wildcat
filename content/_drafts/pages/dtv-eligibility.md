## 5. Spec du Quiz éligibilité DTV

**Nom interne** : `DtvEligibilityQuiz` (îlot interactif minimal, vanilla JS — convention Astro du projet).
**Principe** : 6 questions, chacune attribue un score. Le quiz reflète les **vrais critères DTV** (common_ground) — il ne promet jamais une approbation. Verdict = somme de drapeaux (vert/jaune/rouge), pas une note chiffrée affichée.

**Logique de scoring** : on compte les réponses. Une réponse "rouge" (disqualifiante) force le verdict 3. Sinon : 0 réponse "jaune" → verdict 1 ; 1+ réponse "jaune" → verdict 2.

### Questions

**Q1 — How long do you plan to train Muay Thai in Thailand?**
- a) 6 months or more → vert
- b) 3–5 months → jaune (sous le seuil de la lettre 6 mois)
- c) 1 month or less → **rouge** (cours court = ~99 % de rejet)

**Q2 — Can you show around 500,000 THB in a personal bank account (savings/checking, in your name)?**
- a) Yes, in my personal account → vert
- b) Yes, but the funds are in investment/company/crypto accounts → jaune (à reformater)
- c) No → **rouge**

**Q3 — Are you 20 or older, with a clean immigration & criminal record?**
- a) Yes → vert
- b) Not sure about my record → jaune
- c) Under 20 → **rouge** (peut venir en dépendant d'un parent → message dédié)

**Q4 — Is your passport valid for at least 6 more months?**
- a) Yes → vert
- b) It expires soon → jaune (renouveler avant de postuler)
- c) No / expired → **rouge**

**Q5 — Will you apply from outside Thailand (home country or a neighbouring country)?**
- a) Yes → vert
- b) I'm already in Thailand and unsure → jaune (doit sortir pour postuler)
- c) No, I expected to apply inside Thailand → jaune

**Q6 — Are you applying with family (spouse / children under 20)?**
- a) Just me → vert (neutre)
- b) With spouse and/or children under 20 → vert + note (docs dépendants à prévoir)
- c) With a partner we're not legally married to → jaune (le statut légal est requis pour les dépendants)

### Verdicts (wording EN) + CTA

**Verdict 1 — "You look eligible ✅"**
> Based on your answers, you meet the core DTV requirements for the Muay Thai soft-power route. The next step is the paperwork — your enrolment letter, proof of funds, and the e-Visa application. We can prepare your training documents and walk you through it.
- **CTA** : `Start your DTV application →` vers `/contact` (pré-rempli : sujet "DTV — looks eligible", note quiz). Secondaire : `Chat on WhatsApp`.

**Verdict 2 — "You're probably eligible — let's check a couple of things 🟡"**
> You're close. One or two answers need a closer look (for example: funds sitting in the wrong type of account, or a program shorter than 6 months). These are usually easy to fix before you apply.
- **CTA** : `Ask us anything — we reply on WhatsApp` vers WhatsApp + `/contact` (sujet "DTV — needs review").

**Verdict 3 — "Let's clarify your situation first 🔎"**
> Right now, one requirement looks like a blocker — most often a course under 6 months, missing funds, or applying from inside Thailand. That doesn't always mean no: tell us your situation and we'll give you an honest answer.
> *(Si Q3c < 20 ans : "If you're under 20, you may still be able to join as a dependent on a parent's DTV.")*
- **CTA** : `Tell us your situation` vers `/contact` (sujet "DTV — needs clarification"). **Aucune promesse**, ton chaleureux.

**Règles transverses du quiz**
- Jamais "guaranteed", "you will get the visa", "approved".
- Chaque verdict rappelle en petit : *"This quiz is guidance, not legal advice — final approval is the embassy's decision."*
- Le CTA pré-rempli passe le verdict + un résumé des réponses dans le message `/contact` (champ caché ou querystring) pour que Meaw réponde avec contexte.
