## 2. Snippet block ×3 variantes

**Variante A — décisionnelle (recommandée)**
> There's no visa literally called a "Muay Thai visa." For most foreign trainees today, the right long-stay route is the **Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)** — a 5-year, multiple-entry visa that lets you live in Thailand 180 days per entry with Muay Thai as your qualifying soft-power activity. The older **ED (education) visa** still fits a narrow case: deep, full-time, single-gym immersion. This page compares both honestly, then shows the training documents we issue here in Chiang Mai.

**Variante B — geo + cost**
> Want to train Muay Thai in Thailand for several months? You don't need a special "Muay Thai visa" — you need the **DTV**, which uses Muay Thai as an eligible soft-power activity, runs 180 days per entry over 5 years, and asks for proof of ~500,000 THB in savings. At Wildcat in Chiang Mai we provide the enrollment letter, training schedule and payment receipt your application needs.

**Variante C — honnêteté / anti-arnaque (E-E-A-T)**
> A "Muay Thai visa" is shorthand. In practice you choose between the **DTV** (flexible, 5-year, travel + remote-work friendly) and the **ED visa** (one gym, structured, in-country admin). We won't promise a guaranteed approval — no honest camp can. What we can do is issue clean, accurate training documents from a real Chiang Mai camp, and walk you through the steps. — *Meaw*

## 4. Structure H2 / H3

> H1 : **`Muay Thai Visa in Thailand: DTV vs ED Visa (a Chiang Mai camp's honest guide)`**
> Sous le H1 → snippet block (variante A). Puis disclaimer YMYL en italique (voir §6).

### H2 — Is there really a "Muay Thai visa"? (the soft-power basis camps gloss over)
- Corriger le terme : pas de visa nommé « Muay Thai ». Expliquer la **catégorie soft power / qualifying activity** : depuis le lancement du DTV, le Muay Thai est une **activité Thai soft power éligible** (au même titre que la cuisine thaï, le sport) — c'est *la base légale* qui rend ton entraînement qualifiant.
- Pourquoi c'est important : c'est précisément le point que la plupart des camps **ne** t'expliquent **pas** (gap confirmé : aucune page top SERP ne le couvre). Comprendre la base = remplir le dossier juste.
- Vulgarisation (Module C) : « soft power activity (the cultural/sport categories Thailand actively wants visitors to come and do — Muay Thai is one of them) ».

### H2 — DTV vs ED visa for Muay Thai: the honest comparison

#### H3 — The honest verdict by profile
- **The 3-month trier / first-timer** → tourist visa is enough; don't overpay for a visa you don't need yet.
- **The serious trainee who also works remotely / wants to travel** → **DTV** (the usual winner now).
- **The all-in, full-time, one-gym immersion student who doesn't mind admin** → ED can still make sense.
- **Honnêteté différenciante (sous-angle clé du SERP)** : « Many camps still default-promote the ED visa. For most people the DTV now wins — but here's the narrow case where ED is genuinely better. » C'est le signal de confiance que personne d'autre ne donne.
